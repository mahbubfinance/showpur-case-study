## Overview

Showpur is a trade network and financial operating platform designed for Bangladeshi cottage, micro, small and medium enterprises.

It connects product movement, money movement, inventory, receivables, credit intelligence and business relationships within one digital ecosystem.

The product is being designed around a simple outcome:

> **Help CMSMEs sell more, collect faster and grow with confidence.**

## The Problem

Many small businesses manage products, sales, inventory, customer dues and supplier payments through notebooks, spreadsheets and disconnected applications.

This creates several problems:

* Business owners cannot easily see where their money is
* Receivables and supplier payments are difficult to control
* Producers struggle to find reliable showrooms and retailers
* Showrooms need products but may lack sufficient working capital
* Inventory, accounting and trade activities remain disconnected
* Businesses lack reliable information for credit and operating decisions
* Trust between producers, distributors, showrooms and retailers is difficult to establish

## The Showpur Solution

Showpur is being developed as a three-layer CMSME platform.

### 1. Showpur Business Network

A digital business identity and trade network for producers, distributors, showrooms and retailers.

Core areas include:

* Business profiles and public business pages
* Product discovery and product catalogues
* Customer and supplier connections
* Producer-to-showroom product distribution
* Business verification and trade relationships
* Product allocation, dispatch and sales workflows

### 2. AcShow Financial Operating System

A business financial-management layer designed around the daily operating needs of small businesses.

Core areas include:

* Sales and purchase transactions
* Cash and bank management
* Accounts receivable and payable
* Inventory and stock movements
* Chart of Accounts
* Accounting records and financial reports
* Maker-checker controls for sensitive transactions

### 3. Showpur Intelligence

A financial-intelligence layer that helps business owners understand their current position and make better decisions.

Planned and developing capabilities include:

* Cash-position visibility
* Profit and inventory analysis
* Receivable and payable monitoring
* Business-health indicators
* Cashflow insights and alerts
* Trade-credit assessment
* Business trust and credit intelligence

## Key Product Principle

Showpur is not intended to become another general social network.

The most important business-network indicators are:

* Number of verified buyers
* Number of connected suppliers
* Number of products handled
* Active trade relationships
* Product movement
* Sales and collection performance
* Business reliability and trade trust

Page views and follower counts are not treated as primary business-success metrics.
